# Privacy Policy (summary)

Effective 2026-10-04.

This is a summary. The authoritative, complete text is published on the
website, and **the website text prevails** if it differs from this file:

## Scope

The policy applies to the hosted coordination service at `control.yadori.link`
and to the website `yadori.link`. It does not apply to the open-source
software itself, which runs on your own devices and is licensed under
AGPL-3.0-only (without warranty under that licence). The hosted service
is governed by the Privacy Policy and the
[Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

## Key points

- Your file contents, file names and folder paths are never uploaded to the
  coordination service. Files are transferred between your authorised devices
  end-to-end encrypted; where a direct connection is not possible they pass
  through third-party encrypted relays (iroh public relays).
- The service stores the minimum needed to let your devices find and authorize
  each other: your Google account identifier and email address, your devices
  (name, public signing key, online state, reported network addresses), and
  the folder groups and access rules between them. Private keys stay on the
  device.
- Sign-in uses Google with the `openid` and `email` scopes only. No password
  is stored.
- No ads, analytics or trackers. The website sets no cookies and makes no
  requests to third parties.
- Connections between devices can be direct or pass through public relay
  servers operated by the iroh project. A relay forwards encrypted packets and
  can observe IP addresses, device identifiers, timing and volume, not file
  contents.
- Third parties: Google (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting, database, storage,
  DNS and logs), GitHub (source code and release downloads), and the iroh
  relay servers described above. Beyond these processors, information is
  shown only to the people you share a folder group with (group and device
  names) or provided where required by law.
- The operator's postal address is disclosed without delay on request to the
  contact email.
- Retention: account, device and sharing records are kept while your account
  exists. You can export your data (`yadorilink account export`) and request
  deletion (`yadorilink account delete request`, or "Account & Data..." in the
  desktop app), with a grace period of 7 days by default. Operational logs
  generated by Cloudflare Workers are retained on Cloudflare for at most 7 days.
- Crash and error reporting is disabled on the service.
